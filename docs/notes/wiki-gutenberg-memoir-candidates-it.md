These are Italian texts whose TITLE holds a memoir word (memorie, memoria,
ricordi, ricordo, rimembranze, diario, taccuino, noterelle, lettere,
epistolario, carteggio, autobiografia, vita di, la mia vita, confessioni,
mie prigioni, cronaca, cronache), and which are NOT already in the memoir
group by its subject rule (26 books are; wiki-gutenberg-claude-plan.md §10).*

How to use it:
- Fill **yes/no**. The **suggested** column is Claude's reading of the
  title and subjects only; nothing has been read from the books.
- "now" says where the rule puts the book today. A **yes** puts it in
  the memoir group: it is processed first, and it enters the scope even
  where it is "not in scope" today.
- The list is kept by ebook number. Its sha256 goes into the annotations
  Codex `method` beside the subject rule. A "no" is kept too, so the book
  is not proposed again.
- Notable: **Garibaldi's own *Memorie* (#22)** is not in the memoir group
  by the rule today.
